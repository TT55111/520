# 520 Memorial - Product Context

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
A personal couple memorial website celebrating the relationship between two people (TT & CC). The site serves as a digital love letter: a place to count the days spent together, share photos, leave messages, and play "their song." It's meant to be visited privately by the couple, shared occasionally with close friends, and potentially revisited on anniversaries.

## Users
- **Primary**: The couple themselves (TT & CC), visiting on personal devices (phones, laptops)
- **Secondary**: Close friends who might receive a link on special occasions
- **Context**: Private, emotional, intimate. Not a public social profile. The mood is warm, nostalgic, quietly joyful.

## Brand Tone
- **Voice**: Intimate, poetic, understated. Like a handwritten letter, not a billboard.
- **Mood**: Warm dusk light. The feeling of holding hands. Quiet confidence in the relationship.
- **Anti-references**: 
  - Not "cutesy" (no excessive emojis, no cartoon hearts)
  - Not "corporate" (no SaaS metrics, no conversion funnels)
  - Not "generic romantic" (no rose petals, no diamond rings, no cliché stock imagery)
  - Not "dark mode edgy" (this is light, warm, open)

## Strategic Principles
1. **Restraint over decoration**: Every element must earn its place. If it doesn't deepen the emotional connection, remove it.
2. **Typography as voice**: The fonts should feel like handwriting and poetry, not UI components.
3. **Motion with meaning**: Animations should feel like breathing, not buzzing. Slow, organic, cyclical.
4. **Color as temperature**: The palette should feel like skin warmth, not neon sign.
5. **Personal over perfect**: Slight imperfections (hand-drawn feel, organic shapes) are preferable to clinical precision.

## Scene Sentence
A young couple opens this site on a phone at 9pm, sitting together on a couch, the room dimly lit by a single lamp, showing each other photos from their first trip together.
